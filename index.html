Our Story

Tonny Omi Movers was founded with a simple mission: to provide professional, reliable, and affordable moving services to families and businesses across Kenya. What started as a small operation has grown into a trusted name in the moving industry, thanks to our unwavering commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

We believe that moving should be a positive experience. Our mission is to take the stress out of relocation by providing transparent pricing, professional service, and personalized attention to every client. Whether you're moving around the corner or across the country, we treat your move with the same level of care and dedication.

Our Values

Honesty & Integrity: We're transparent about our pricing and services. No hidden fees, no surprises—just honest communication from start to finish.

Quality & Care: Your belongings are valuable to you. We handle them with the same care we'd give to our own possessions.

Professional Excellence: Our team is trained, experienced, and dedicated to delivering exceptional service every single time.

Customer-First Approach: Your satisfaction is our priority. We listen to your needs and tailor our services accordingly.

Meet the Founder

Anthony Peter is the visionary behind Tonny Omi Movers. With extensive experience in the logistics and moving industry, Anthony founded the company with a passion for providing honest, professional moving services to the people of Kenya. His commitment to customer service and operational excellence has made Tonny Omi Movers a household name in Nairobi and beyond.

When he's not overseeing moves, Anthony is dedicated to training his team, ensuring every customer interaction reflects the company's core values of professionalism, care, and integrity.
